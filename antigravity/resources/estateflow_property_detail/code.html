About This Property

Perched atop Summit Ridge, The Glass Pavilion represents the pinnacle of modern architectural achievement. This newly constructed estate offers an unparalleled living experience, characterized by seamless indoor-outdoor integration and sweeping views of the Los Angeles basin.

The main level features an expansive open-concept living area with automated floor-to-ceiling fleetwood doors. The chef's kitchen is appointed with dual marble islands, state-of-the-art Miele appliances, and a concealed catering prep area.

The primary suite serves as a private sanctuary, encompassing an entire wing with dual spa-like bathrooms, bespoke dressing rooms, and a private terrace overlooking the zero-edge infinity pool.
